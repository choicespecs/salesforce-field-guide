Sales & Service processes

The standard objects encode a sales process and a support process. Knowing the intended flow helps you configure it, and explains a lot of built-in behaviour.

Leads

  • Sources: web forms (Web-to-Lead generates an HTML form that posts into Salesforce), imports, marketing automation, API.
  • Status tracks qualification (Open → Working → Qualified / Unqualified). One status is flagged as the "converted" status.
  • Lead assignment rules route new leads to owners or queues.

Lead conversion

Converting a lead creates (or matches to existing) an Account and Contact, and optionally an Opportunity. The lead becomes read-only with IsConverted = true.

  • Map custom lead fields to account, contact and opportunity fields in Object Manager → Lead → Fields → Map Lead Fields. Unmapped data is left behind on the converted lead.
  • Campaign history and activities move to the contact.

Opportunities

  • Stages are picklist values, each with a default probability and forecast category (Pipeline, Best Case, Commit, Closed, Omitted). Edit them in Object Manager → Opportunity → Fields → Stage.
  • Sales processes choose which stages each record type uses.
  • IsClosed and IsWon are derived from the stage's type (Open / Closed-Won / Closed-Lost).
  • Opportunity Contact Roles record who's involved (Decision Maker, Influencer…) and are the link between contacts and deals for campaign influence.
  • Opportunity Teams and Splits share credit across reps.
  • Path + Kanban list views are the standard way reps move deals through stages.

Products, price books and quotes

  1. Create Product2 records (the catalogue).
  2. Add each product to the Standard Price Book (required first) with a list price.
  3. Create custom price books (e.g. "EMEA 2027", "Partners") with their own PricebookEntry prices.
  4. On an opportunity, pick one price book, then add Opportunity Products (OpportunityLineItem) with quantity and sales price. The opportunity's Amount becomes the sum of line totals.
  5. Quotes copy the line items, can be synced back to the opportunity, and generate PDFs from templates. Complex configuration, discounting and approvals call for CPQ / Revenue Cloud.

Forecasting and territories

  • Collaborative Forecasts roll up opportunity amounts (or quantities, or splits) by forecast category, period and the forecast hierarchy (usually the role hierarchy). Managers can adjust subordinates' numbers.
  • Enterprise Territory Management assigns accounts to territories by rules (geography, industry, size) independently of the role hierarchy, and grants access accordingly.

Cases

  • Origin records the channel; Status values are flagged open or closed; Priority drives routing and SLAs.
  • Support processes pick which statuses each case record type uses.
  • Case Feed shows emails, posts, calls and field changes in one timeline.
  • Parent cases group duplicates or related issues (ParentId).

Routing and SLAs

Case assignment rules

Evaluate criteria on new cases and assign to a user or queue.

Auto-response rules

Send the customer an acknowledgement email using a template chosen by criteria.

Escalation rules

Reassign or notify when a case stays open past a time limit, respecting business hours.

Omni-Channel

Pushes work to agents based on their presence status and capacity. Queue-based routing sends from queues; skills-based routing matches case attributes (language, product) to agent skills.

Entitlements & milestones

An entitlement says what support a customer gets; an entitlement process defines milestones (First Response within 1 hour, Resolution within 1 business day) with warning and violation actions.

Business hours & holidays

Used by escalation rules, milestones and BusinessHours.diff() in Apex.

Agent productivity

  • Service Console: a tabbed Lightning app showing a case, its contact and account side by side, with a utility bar.
  • Knowledge: articles with versions, publication status, data categories for visibility, and channels (internal, customer, partner, public). Attach articles to cases.
  • Macros run several actions with one click (send templated email, update status, post to feed).
  • Quick Text: reusable snippets inserted into emails and chats.
  • Case merge combines duplicates.

Activities and email

  • Tasks (to-dos, logged calls, emails) and Events (calendar) relate to a person (WhoId: Lead or Contact) and a thing (WhatId: Account, Opportunity, Case, custom object).
  • With Shared Activities, one activity can relate to up to 50 contacts (stored in TaskRelation/EventRelation).
  • Einstein Activity Capture syncs email and calendar from Gmail/Outlook; captured items aren't always stored as standard activity records, which affects reporting.
